Help & FAQs
goes a long way.

Which password do I use?

Use the password for buddy-phone in EMQX Authentication. This is different from your EMQX account login, the buddy-device password on your ESP32, and your Gemini API key. If your username differs, change it under Connection details.

Why does it say “login rejected”?

The message service refused your username or password. Check the buddy-phone credential in EMQX, then edit your connection. Passwords are case-sensitive; avoid copying extra spaces. Buddy won’t repeatedly retry a rejected login.

Login worked. Where’s Buddy?

Your phone reached EMQX, but the ESP32 hasn’t replied. Keep Buddy powered, on Wi-Fi, and running V4.5 or later with cloud setup completed. Check that both use Buddy ID mous-buddy. The serial message “Cloud connection ready” confirms its broker login.

What if I can’t connect?

Check your internet and broker address, then try another network. Some networks block secure WebSocket port 8084. Check that your EMQX deployment is running, hasn’t reached its free allowance, and permits publish/subscribe on deskbuddy/mous-buddy/#. Keep Spend Limit at zero.

Will I need to connect every time?

Leave “Remember this device” on. After a successful connection, this browser remembers Buddy and reconnects automatically. Bookmark your usual production Vercel address; a new preview URL uses separate browser storage. Private browsing, clearing website data, a new browser or a changed password can require connecting again. Disconnect pauses this; Forget removes the saved connection.

How does voice work?

Select English beside the microphone for English dictation. Hold, wait for Listening, speak clearly, and release. Unfinished words, early stops or reported uncertainty stay for editing instead of sending part of a sentence. Turn on Review dictation before sending in Voice & preferences to check every result. Safari supplies the recognition; it can still mishear final words, names and numbers. Allow speech access on first use, then hold again. Siri/Dictation and internet may be needed; keyboard dictation also works.

Why did my message not send?

Messages need a live Buddy and a completed speech result. Cancelled speech, leaving the page or losing the connection sends nothing. If delivery wasn’t confirmed, check the conversation and timer before trying again: Buddy may already have received the command.

Why isn’t every answer instant?

Simple greetings, time and available weather can be answered on the device. General questions wait for Gemini and the internet. Watch Buddy’s thinking indicator; there’s no fixed 30-second wait.

What is remembered?

Your saved connection is encrypted in this browser’s IndexedDB, with a non-exportable browser key. That does not protect it from malicious scripts on this same site or someone using your unlocked browser. It isn’t uploaded to GitHub. Phone speech services, EMQX and Gemini process the relevant audio/text. Chat is not saved by this dashboard between page loads; Buddy keeps four recent exchanges until cleared or restarted.

What can Buddy look up?

Firmware 4.8 adds Batangas City daily weather for the previous seven days and next seven days, sourced from Open-Meteo. Past days are model estimates, not local station measurements. Ask explicitly for Philippines, world, technology or science news to load up to three GMA RSS headlines; links appear with the reply. These are headlines, not full-article reporting or unrestricted web search. Requests can fail or use clearly marked cached data. A local calculator checks numerical expressions without an AI request when you type “calculate 2+2”. Technical explanations still use Gemini’s knowledge.

Why does Buddy nap?

With firmware 4.7, real inactivity leads to bored eyes, a yawn, then a visual nap after three minutes. Network heartbeats don’t keep him awake. A message or BOOT tap wakes him; Wi-Fi and timers keep running. These are simulated emotions. New faces and teaching guidance require uploading firmware 4.7.

Where is my learning saved?

Only in this browser on this device. Use Notebook & review to export or merge a backup. Saved cards can be reviewed offline. Guided lessons need Buddy online. A new conversation clears recent chat context, so choose Teach me again to resume a topic. AI explanations can be wrong: check an answer before saving it.

How do I keep it free?

Use Vercel Hobby, keep EMQX Serverless Spend Limit at 0 and Gemini billing disabled. Each provider has allowances. Service can pause when a free allowance is used up.
